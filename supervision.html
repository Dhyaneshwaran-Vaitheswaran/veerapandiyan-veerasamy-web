Supervision & Opportunities

Mentorship Philosophy

Dr. Veera is deeply committed to nurturing the next generation of engineers and researchers. His supervision philosophy bridges cutting-edge academic theory with rigorous industrial reality. Students in his group gain hands-on experience with industrial automation, cyber-physical security frameworks, and advanced real-time testing platforms.

Research Environment & Student Benefits

By joining Dr. Veerasamy's research team, students actively engage with:

Industry-Standard Platforms

Hands-on development using DIgSILENT PowerFactory, TwinCAT/Beckhoff PLCs, and OPAL-RT/RTDS real-time simulators.

Accredited Methodologies

Training aligned with ISO/IEC 17025 laboratory quality frameworks and national/international standards testing.

Interdisciplinary Skills

Mastery over cyber-physical systems, blending robust power engineering with Explainable AI and cybersecurity.

If you are highly motivated to work on the future of secure, sustainable energy infrastructure and possess a strong background in electrical engineering, control systems, or computer science, please reach out with your CV and a brief research statement via email.

How to apply: Send your CV, transcripts, and a brief statement of research interests to my email via Veerapandiyan.Veerasamy@curtin.edu.sg or veerapandian220@gmail.com.
