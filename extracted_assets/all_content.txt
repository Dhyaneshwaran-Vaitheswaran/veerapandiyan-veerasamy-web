About me
Biography
Dr. Veerapandiyan Veerasamy
 is a Lecturer in Cybersecurity at Curtin University, Singapore, specializing in the intersection of cyber-resilience, artificial intelligence, and next-generation energy infrastructure.
Prior to his current appointment, he served as a Research Fellow at the Electrification and Power Grid Centre (EPGC) / Energy Research Inst
itute (ERI@N)
 at 
Nanyang Technological University (NTU)
, Singapore. During his five-year tenure at NTU, he successfully executed high-impact, nationally funded research initiatives spanning smart grids, virtual power plants (VPPs), distributed energy resources (DERs), and digital energy systems.
Dr. Veera’s
 research is defined by its strong translation from theory to practice. He possesses deep field expertise in the operation, maintenance, and technical standard compliance testing of 
MW-scale microgrids (both AC and DC systems)
 and 
MWh-scale Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)
. He is a specialist in Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL, C-HIL, P-HIL) real-time simulations using OPAL-RT and RTDS platforms.
A recognized technical authority, he contributes directly to regional and international standardization, offering deep expertise in compliance and regulatory frameworks including 
IEEE 1547, IEEE 2030, IEC 62898, IEC 61851, TR25, TR77
, and the 
EMA Transmission Code
. Notably, he brings extensive hands-on experience working within 
SAC-SINGLAS certified laboratory
 environments specifically for Electric Vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure and Energy Storage System (ESS) validation. Formally trained in 
ISO/IEC 17025 compliance
, 
Dr. Veera
 bridges the gap between cutting-edge academic research and rigorous, industry-accredited testing and quality management frameworks.

Research Interests
Infrastructure Cyber-Resilience:
 Cyber-physical security architectures, blockchain applications, and resilient control systems for critical energy infrastructure.
Applied Artificial Intelligence
: 
Explainable AI (XAI) and neural network architectures for energy network monitoring, optimal sensor placement, and predictive control.
Next-Gen Power Systems:
 Grid integration of renewables, hierarchical control of DC microgrids, and standard-compliant V2G/V2X implementations.
Core Technical Competencies
Accredited Testing &amp; Quality Assurance:
 Deep operational familiarity with SAC-SINGLAS accredited laboratory environments; comprehensive training in ISO/IEC 17025 general requirements for the competence of testing and calibration laboratories.
E-Mobility &amp; Grid Compliance:
 End-to-end safety, interoperability, and performance testing of EV charging stations (TR25 / IEC 61851) and utility-scale Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).
Advanced Real-Time Emulation: 
Complex Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL, Control-HIL, Power-HIL) implementation on OPAL-RT and RTDS platforms for power system stability and cyber-physical resilience audits.
Industrial Automation:
 Industrial control programming, data logging, and communication protocols (Modbus, TwinCAT, Beckhoff PLCs).

Teaching
Dr. Veera is deeply committed to nurturing the next generation of engineers and researchers. His supervision philosophy bridges cutting-edge academic theory with rigorous industrial reality. Students in his group gain hands-on experience with industrial automation, cyber-physical security frameworks, and advanced real-time testing platforms.
Research Environment &amp; Student Benefits
By joining Dr. Veerasamy&#39;s research team, students actively engage with:
Industry-Standard Platforms:
 Hands-on development using 
DIgSILENT PowerFactory
, 
TwinCAT/Beckhoff PLCs
, and 
OPAL-RT/RTDS
 real-time simulators.
Accredited Methodologies:
 Training aligned with 
ISO/IEC 17025
 laboratory quality frameworks and national/international standards testing (TR25, TR77, IEEE, IEC).
Interdisciplinary Skills:
 Mastery over cyber-physical systems, blending robust power engineering with 
Explainable AI (XAI)
, machine learning, and cybersecurity architectures.
