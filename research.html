Research & Publications

Research Interests

Infrastructure Cyber-Resilience

Cyber-physical security architectures, blockchain applications, and resilient control systems for critical energy infrastructure.

Applied Artificial Intelligence

Explainable AI (XAI) and neural network architectures for energy network monitoring, optimal sensor placement, and predictive control.

Next-Gen Power Systems

Grid integration of renewables, hierarchical control of DC microgrids, and standard-compliant V2G/V2X implementations.

Core Technical Competencies

Accredited Testing & QA

Deep operational familiarity with SAC-SINGLAS accredited laboratory environments; comprehensive training in ISO/IEC 17025 testing requirements.

E-Mobility & Grid Compliance

End-to-end safety, interoperability, and performance testing of EV charging stations (TR25 / IEC 61851) and utility-scale Battery Energy Storage Systems.

Advanced Real-Time Emulation

Complex Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL, C-HIL, P-HIL) implementation on OPAL-RT and RTDS platforms for system stability and cyber-resilience audits.

Industrial Automation

Industrial control programming, data logging, and communication protocols including Modbus, TwinCAT, and Beckhoff PLCs.
